# 🚀 VDB Organizer

We are excited to introduce the first public release of **VDB Organizer** (Vplayer) — an all-in-one, privacy-focused media organizer, player, and creative toolkit designed for Windows. 

Built with **Tauri v2 + Rust** and a modern **React/TypeScript** interface, VDB Organizer gives you full control over your media collections with zero telemetry and lightning-fast local performance.

---

### ✨ Key Features

#### 📂 Universal Media Player & Document Viewer
* **40+ Formats Supported**: Seamlessly open videos (MP4, MKV, WEBM, MOV, AVI), audio (MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, OGG), graphics/design assets (PSD, TIFF, EPS, CDR, SVG, PNG, JPG, WebP), documents/books (PDF, EPUB, TXT, MD), archives (ZIP, 7Z, RAR, VDB), and fonts (TTF, WOFF2).
* **Deep Archive Exploration**: Browse and extract compressed archives without external utilities.

#### 🖼️ High-Performance Gallery & Organization
* **Dynamic Justified Gallery**: Virtualized rendering for butter-smooth scrolling through thousands of items.
* **Dual-Pane Split-Screen (`S`)**: Compare files and directories side-by-side with synchronized navigation and instant handoff.
* **Instant Search & Tagging**: Ultra-fast file lookups integrated with Windows filesystem and Everything IPC, complete with tags, ratings, and notes.

#### 🤖 Local AI & Creative Utilities
* **Local AI Background Removal**: 1-click cutout powered by BiRefNet/BEN2 models — 100% offline with zero cloud upload.
* **Lossless Media Trimmer**: Quick clipping and segment export for audio and video files.
* **Batch Image Converter**: Multi-threaded format converter & optimizer (WebP, PNG, JPEG, AVIF) with lossless/quality controls.
* **Batch Renaming & Regex**: Search, replace, regex patterns, and sequential auto-numbering.
* **Desktop Snip Tool (`Alt+P`)**: Instant screenshot and region clipping directly into your library.

#### 🌐 Streaming & Media Downloader
* **Local LAN Media Server**: Pair mobile devices or smart TVs instantly via QR code to stream video and music over your local Wi-Fi.
* **Media Downloader**: Download high-resolution video (up to 4K/8K) and extract audio from 1,000+ platforms.
* **Integrated Web Browser**: Sandboxed Chromium WebView2 browser with built-in ad/tracker blocking (uBlock Origin) and 1-click media archiving.

#### 🎙️ Hands-Free Voice & Windows Integration
* **Offline Voice Navigation**: Private offline speech recognition (Vosk acoustic engine) for hands-free playback and gallery navigation.
* **Glass UI & Transparent Mode**: Adjustable window opacity (10%–100%) and backdrop blur for floating reference workflows.
* **Full Keyboard Command Center**: Ergonomic hotkeys (`F` to toggle panels, `H` for badge toggles, `Space` for playback, zoom gestures).

### 📦 Installation

1. Download the Windows installer (`vdb-organizer_0.1.0_x64.exe`) from the assets below.
2. Run the installer and launch **VDB Organizer**.
3. Press <kbd>ESC</kbd> or open the in-app **Quick Guide** anytime for keyboard shortcuts and workflow tips.
